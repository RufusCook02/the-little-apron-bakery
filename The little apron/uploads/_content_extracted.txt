OUR STORY PAGE
Header
Every Baker Starts Somewhere
The Little Pink Apron
Every passion starts small.
Mine started in the kitchen wearing a tiny pink apron, baking alongside my mum and dreaming about cakes, cafes and creating something special.
Those early days sparked a love for baking that would follow me for years to come.
Learning the Craft
As my passion grew, so did my curiosity to learn more… but being from a small rural town there was no opportunity to take lessons in my area.
That’s when my Mum reached out and I began taking baking lessons from a family friend, followed by fondant cake decorating classes from another local and, discovered the endless possibilities of cake design and creativity.
What began as a hobby was slowly turning into something more.
The Sugar Shed
One of the biggest turning points in my journey was working at The Sugar Shed, a dessert restaurant in Pukekohe. The best first job a baking gal could ask for.
Here I learnt the art of buttercream cakes, refined my decorating skills, and truly discovered the joy of creating desserts that satisfied the ultimate sweet tooth. 
Baking for Loved Ones
For years, baking remained something I did for family and friends.
Birthday cakes, celebration cakes, cupcakes for special moments — each one made with love.
What started as “just helping out” soon became something people kept asking for.
CushsCreations
Eventually, I began sharing my cakes online through CushsCreations.
Posting my creations allowed me to explore my creativity and bring people's cake ideas to life.
Seeing how much joy a cake could bring someone quickly became the most rewarding part.
A Dream of Cafes
Growing up, my mum and I spent countless hours in cafes.
We would always talk about what we loved, what made a place feel special, and what we would do differently one day.
Owning something creative and welcoming had always been a dream.
The Little Apron
The Little Apron is where everything came together.
A place where I can:
• Create beautiful custom cakes • Bring customers' visions to life • Teach kids and adults the joy of baking and decorating • Share creativity through workshops and experiences
Because sometimes the sweetest memories are made in the kitchen.
