OUR STORY
Hi, I’m Cushla [handwritten font]
Chief baker, decorator, administrator, content creator and more for The Little Apron. There was once a time, when I was just a little girl learning to bake.
Where it started [photo of me on the step ladder]
*Arrow* 
And it grew to something more, when I wanted to take classes but there weren’t many around, so we reached out. There were many people who took the time to teach me properly.
[fondant making birthday event, fondant cakes, fire truck cake, owls, Mums cake]
Then, at 15, I landed my dream first job at a dessert restaurant called The Sugar Shed.
[photo of Liv and I, testing the food, some of the cakes we created with Sugar Shed in background)
That’s where I learnt the art of buttercream cakes 
*Arrow* 
I saved up my first few paychecks and bought my very first stand mixer. [photo of me with my stand mixer
For the last 5 years, this has all lived under CushsCreations.
[photo of cush in CushCreations apron]
Then The Little Apron was born, representing a new chapter and most importantly a new apron. From being that kid in the kitchen, to learning, to working, to where I am now. The brand signifies the journey I’ve been on, those involved and the love I have for it.
